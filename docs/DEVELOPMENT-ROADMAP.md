# Model Lab development programme

## 0.8: assumptions and simulation

Implement and evaluate the three laboratories in RELEASE-0.8-CONTRACT.md. The
scientific experiment owns the generating mechanism, target, methods, records
and Monte Carlo uncertainty. Research views, numerical inspection and teaching
refer to that same object. Existing models establish the workflow before new
families are added. Participant and physical-Windows evaluation were unavailable
under the user's subsequent instruction; retain that limitation in release claims.

## 0.9: econometric identification and file-backed research

Add a pinned PyFixest adapter for fixed effects and IV. Verify within projection,
partialling-out and 2SLS arithmetic against analytic cases and independent R
references. Include weak-identification cases and appropriate inference rather
than treating first-stage diagnostics as instrument validity. Introduce hashed,
project-relative Arrow files and paged views through the existing Python adapter;
raise dataset bounds only after hardware-specific memory/performance tests.

## 0.10: causal ML and research decisions

Add DoubleML partially linear and binary-treatment ATE workflows using existing
ridge and forest learners. Expose actual cross-fitted nuisance predictions,
residuals, score contributions and supported sensitivity analyses. Bound and
record alternative specifications. Do not pool incomparable estimands or
populations, interpret specification spread as a confidence interval, or silently
select favorable results. Teach association, prediction and intervention as
different quantities.

## 1.0: independently used research and teaching software

Require actual research and instructor use, lesson authoring, accessible native
interaction, stable adapter contracts, supported distribution and independent
replication. Evaluate usability descriptively before claiming educational gains;
efficacy requires a separate adequate study. A model that executes is not
automatically inspected or fully taught. Preserve these capability distinctions.

PCA, kernels, neural and Bayesian models enter through complete use cases, each
with its own geometry, diagnostics and tests. Bayesian computation requires
sampler diagnostics, predictive checks and calibration. No universal-method
claim follows from a larger menu. No new hosted service or paid model provider
is required for this programme.
