## What to watch for

**The `mtry` prediction.** Students expect "more information is better".
The reveal shows the measured difference from a real second fit; discuss
why a forest can lose from letting every tree see everything, and why the
honest answer on a particular dataset is "measure it".

**Importance as cause.** When a student says vacancy "drives" the rate,
point at the generating mechanism in `examples/generate.py`: the
coefficients are known here because the data are synthetic, which is the
only situation in which they can be. Ask what would be needed on real data.
