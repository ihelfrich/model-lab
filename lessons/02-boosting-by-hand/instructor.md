## What to watch for

**A wrong direction on the first prediction.** The student who says the
prediction for $x = 2$ goes up in round 2 has usually looked at the outcome
(4) rather than the residual. Ask: after round 1 the prediction at $x = 2$
is {{byhand.round.0.after.1}}; is that above or below 4? The residual's sign
is the direction of the next move, every time.

**The rate 1.0 discussion.** Let the student set the rate to 1.0 and watch
the loss hit zero. Then ask what they would predict at $x = 2.5$, and at
$x = 10$. The model has no opinion beyond the two leaves it has seen. This
is the moment to say the words "training loss" and "validation loss" out
loud and point at the next lesson.
