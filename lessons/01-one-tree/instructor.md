## What to watch for

**The prediction of the threshold.** Students who have met regression first
often pick 1.5 or 3.5 because "the big jump is between 4 and 8" looks like
a slope argument. Have them compute the two groups' means and squared
errors by hand for one outer cut; the table in the lesson shows all three
once the experiment has run.

**Depth 2 as a success.** When the training SSE hits zero, some students
are pleased. Ask what the tree predicts at $x = 2.5$ exactly, then at
$x = 100$. The lesson's point is that a zero training loss on four points
is a description of the table, not of the world.
